### **Analysis of Current Site vs. Proposed Plan**

This analysis identifies inconsistencies and gaps between the existing `sharkcitydrum.com` and the new application plan, ensuring we account for all intended functionality.

#### **1. Key Differences in Navigation & Structure:**

*   **"Shop" and "Book Online" Pages:**
    *   **Current Site:** Has explicit navigation links for "Shop" and "Book Online". While these pages may not be fully built out, their presence indicates a clear intent for these functionalities.
    *   **Proposed Plan:** Our initial plan does not include dedicated pages for "Shop" or "Book Online". This is the most significant inconsistency.
    *   **Recommendation:** Acknowledge these as critical **Phase 2 features**.
        *   **"Shop"** will require an e-commerce integration (e.g., Stripe, Shopify Lite) and a more complex UI/backend.
        *   **"Book Online"** should be implemented as a detailed inquiry form on the "Contact" page for Phase 1. This form can be designed to capture all necessary booking information (event type, date, location, contact person) and will feed directly into the future CRM.

*   **"Menus" Page:**
    *   **Current Site:** Has a navigation link titled "Menus". This appears to be a leftover artifact from the Wix template and does not contain relevant content.
    *   **Proposed Plan:** We will correctly omit this page.

*   **Sponsors/Partnerships:**
    *   **Current Site:** Displays a list of sponsors near the bottom of the page.
    *   **Proposed Plan:** Our initial page plan did not explicitly place the sponsors.
    *   **Recommendation:** We will add a dedicated "Our Sponsors & Partners" section to the `app_footer.dart` widget. This ensures they are prominently and consistently displayed on every page without cluttering the main navigation. The content for this has been captured above.

#### **2. Content & Feature Enhancements in the New Plan:**

*   **Dedicated Media Page:** Our plan includes a dedicated `/media` page with tabs for photos and videos. This is a major improvement over the current site's simple embedded video section, allowing for a much richer and more organized display of content.

*   **Dedicated Join Us Page:** Our plan includes a `/join-us` page. This creates a clear call to action for prospective members, which is more effective than blending this information with general content.

*   **Structured "About Us" Page:** Our plan consolidates the "Mission" and "Meet the Team" sections into a single, cohesive `/about` page. This provides a clearer narrative for visitors wanting to learn about the organization's purpose and people.

*   **Dynamic Events System:** While the current site shows "No events", our plan is built around a dynamic `events` collection in Firestore. This will allow SCD staff to easily add, update, and display upcoming and past performances, making the site a timely and valuable resource.

#### **3. Conclusion & Actionable Items:**

*   The proposed new site structure is a significant improvement in terms of user experience, organization, and scalability.
*   **Action Item 1:** For the initial launch (Phase 1), the "Book Online" functionality will be handled via an expanded, detailed contact form on the `/contact` page.
*   **Action Item 2:** The "Shop" functionality will be deferred to a future development phase due to its complexity.
*   **Action Item 3:** The list of sponsors will be integrated into the site-wide footer.
*   The static content document above has been updated to reflect all these findings.
